Your expenses stay with you

Xpense only uses your data to run Xpense: to sign you in, show your expenses and sync them between your devices.

  • Sign in your waySign in with Apple, or a one-time code sent to your email.
  • Receipts stay on your iPhoneReceipt photos are never uploaded.
  • No ads, no selling dataNo tracking across other apps and websites. Shared groups are visible only to their members.
  • Delete or export any timeSettings, Account, Delete Account removes your account and data from our servers. Export to CSV from Settings.
