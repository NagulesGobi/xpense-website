Deleting your account

Open Xpense, then Settings, Account, Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and data from our servers.

Exporting your expenses

Settings, Export downloads your expenses as a CSV file.

Receipt photos and uploads

No. Receipt photos stay on your iPhone.

Sync between devices

Check you are signed in to the same account and online, then pull to refresh. If it still doesn’t sync, contact support.

Hide My Email and our replies

Yes. Apple’s private relay forwards our emails to you. To reach us, write from any address.

Reporting a shared group

Use the report option in the app, or email us with the group name and what happened. We review reports promptly.
